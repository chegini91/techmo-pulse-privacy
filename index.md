# Techmo Pulse — Privacy Policy

_Effective: October 20, 2026_

Techmo Pulse shows information about your phone (battery, CPU, memory, network, sensors and more), can show it
in a floating widget, and can keep a log of it. It also lists the latest phone reviews from the Techmo YouTube channel.
The app has no account and no analytics. **The readings it shows and records (battery, sensors, logs, charts,
network details and so on) stay on your device**: the app never sends them to the developer or to anyone else. The
app does show ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some device information for that, as
described under "Ads" below.

## What the app stores on your device

| Data | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Sensor log (battery %, temperature, signal level, CPU %, RAM, power, with timestamps) | The "Sensor log" feature (off until you turn it on) | Until you clear it in the app or uninstall; capped at about 5 MB (oldest entries are removed) |
| Chart history (per-minute averages of the same values, plus mobile signal strength and network speed) | The 1 h / 24 h / 7 d charts | 7 days, or until you clear it in Settings |
| Settings and hardware-test results | Remembering your choices | Until you uninstall |
| Techmo review video list and thumbnails | Showing the Reviews tab, the Dashboard's new-video banner and the Techmo review home-screen widget quickly and offline | Until the next update of the list; thumbnails can be removed by Android or by clearing the app's cache |
| Today's download/upload totals | The "today" figures on the Dashboard | Reset every day; until you uninstall |
| Your ad consent choices | Remembering what you chose in the consent form (see "Ads") | Until you change them or uninstall |
| Whether you bought Pro | Unlocking Pro, also offline (see "Purchases") | Until you uninstall; re-checked with Google Play |
| Data stored by Google's ad software (identifiers and settings it needs to show ads) | Showing ads (see "Ads") | Managed by Google; deleted when you uninstall |

The sensor log, chart history and traffic totals are **excluded from Android backups**. Only settings, test results
and your ad consent choices may be included in your own Google backup, if you have backups turned on.

## Reviews tab

The Reviews tab, the new-video banner on the Dashboard and the Techmo review home-screen widget (if you add it) download the public video list of the Techmo YouTube channel's "Phone Comparison" playlist and the video thumbnails from YouTube
(youtube.com and ytimg.com). Like any website you open, YouTube receives your IP address and basic connection details
with these requests; no other information about you or your device is sent. Opening a video hands it to the
YouTube app or your browser, where Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

## Ads

When there is no new Techmo video to show, the Dashboard shows a banner ad from **Google AdMob** (Google Ireland
Limited in the EU/EEA and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere). To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud,
Google's ad software in the app collects and sends to Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP
address (and the approximate location derived from it), device model and Android version, app information, and how
you interact with the ads. Google uses this data under its own privacy policy, as an independent controller. The
developer does not receive this information about you, only aggregated reports from Google (for example how many
ads were shown).

In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before showing ads, using Google's consent form,
and you can change your choices at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. Depending on what you choose,
Google shows personalised ads, ads that use less data (non-personalised or "limited" ads), or no ads at all; the app
works the same either way. Elsewhere, you can limit ad personalisation by resetting or deleting your advertising ID
in your phone's settings (usually under Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

How Google uses data from apps that show its ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases

Techmo Pulse Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through **Google Play**. Google Play handles the payment and
your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer never sees them. The app only asks Google
Play whether your Google account owns Pro, and remembers the answer on your device. With Pro there are no ads, so
Google's ad software is not started and the ad consent form is not shown.

## Permissions and what they are used for

- **Location** (optional, asked on the Network tab): Android requires it to read the name of the Wi-Fi network you
  are connected to. The name is only shown on screen; it is never stored or sent anywhere. The app does not use
  GPS or track your location.
- **Phone state** (Android 11 and older only): to show the mobile network type (e.g. LTE).
- **Microphone** (optional, only when you run the microphone test): the sound level is measured in memory for a
  few seconds. Nothing is recorded or saved.
- **Display over other apps**: to show the floating widget.
- **Notifications**: the status notification shown while the floating widget or sensor log is on.
- **Internet / network state**: to load the Reviews list and the Dashboard ad (see above). Android also only reports
  the current network (Wi-Fi, mobile data, IP addresses) to apps that declare these. No other internet connections
  are made.
- **Advertising ID**: used by Google AdMob for ads, as described under "Ads".
- **Run at startup, foreground service, wake lock**: to restart the floating widget or sensor log after a reboot
  (only if you turned them on) and, only if you turn on "Keep logging while screen is off", to keep logging with the
  screen off.
- **Vibration, camera flash**: for the vibration and flashlight tests. The camera itself is never used.

## Sharing

Apart from the ads described above, the app only shares data when **you** choose to: exporting the sensor log or the
chart history, or saving or sharing a device report.
You pick where it goes (a folder or another app). A report contains device details such as the model, Android
build, IP addresses and Wi-Fi network name, so review it before sharing it with others.

## Deleting your data

Use **Clear** on the sensor log card, **Clear history** in Settings, or **Reset results** on the Tests tab.
Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

## Your rights

The readings the app records never leave your device, so you control them fully: you can view, export and delete
them in the app (see "Deleting your data"). For data collected by Google for ads, you can change or withdraw your
consent in **Settings → Ad privacy choices** at any time, and exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection and
so on) with Google at https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you live in the EU/EEA or the UK, you also have the
right to complain to your data protection authority. For any question about this policy, contact the developer
(see "Contact").

## Children

The app is not directed at children. The developer collects no personal information from anyone; the ads are
provided by Google as described above.
